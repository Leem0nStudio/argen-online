# PROCEDURAL WORLD

Procedural generation is a gameplay system.

World generation should consider:

- topology
- climate
- biomes
- water
- resources
- settlements
- roads
- danger
- travel cost
- trade
- factions
- dungeons
- chokepoints
- player traffic
- economic connectivity

The generator should avoid purely decorative randomness.

Prefer deterministic seeded generation where reproducibility is useful.

When changing world generation, validate both visual output and gameplay
properties.
